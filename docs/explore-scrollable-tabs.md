# Explore scrollable tabs

Explore exposes creative, contextual, and utility intents in one horizontal tab strip.

- No utility intent is hidden behind a grouped “附近服务” entry.
- The strip remains horizontally scrollable on narrow screens.
- Regional themes may append to the strip without replacing stable intents.
- Selecting a utility tab directly updates the Explore query with one tap.
- Search shortcuts remain available independently and do not replace the tab strip.
